# SHIV Regime V1 — May–June 2026 evidence

Strategy: Trend Pullback/Reclaim + Sideways Edge Reversal.

Method: 5m classifies TREND/RANGE; 15m confirms trend direction; ATM±2 premium/OI confirms; 1m supplies the post-signal entry trigger. Range entries are allowed only near range edges. Maximum 3 trades/day and 20-minute cooldown.

Benchmark: +10 NIFTY points before -5 within 15 minutes. If target and stop occur in the same 1-minute candle, the trade is counted as a loss.

Public-data test window: 2026-05-01 through 2026-06-30. Valid near-expiry sessions available in source: 18.

## Results

- Overall: 40 trades, 15 wins, 25 losses, 0 timeouts, 37.50% win rate.
- Trend: 39 trades, 14 wins, 25 losses, 35.90% win rate.
- Trend CE: 17 trades, 5 wins, 12 losses, 29.41% win rate.
- Trend PE: 22 trades, 9 wins, 13 losses, 40.91% win rate.
- Range: 1 trade, 1 win, 0 losses, 100.00% win rate, but the sample is too small to infer a real range win rate.
- Range CE: 0 trades.
- Range PE: 1 trade, 1 win.

Additional evidence:
- Overall +5 favorable excursion hit rate: 50.00%.
- Overall +10 favorable excursion hit rate: 37.50%.
- Average MFE: 8.22 NIFTY points.
- Average MAE: 6.10 NIFTY points.
- Trend CE average MAE: 7.69 points.
- Trend PE average MAE: 5.08 points.

Conclusion: This fixed V1 does not meet a high-precision acceptance bar and should not be merged into live SHIV. The trend component is weak under the +10/-5 benchmark. The sideways component fired only once, so it requires a separate, better-sampled research design before any conclusion about range trading.
